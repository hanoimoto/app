Documents & License (Giấy tờ & Bằng lái)

To legally drive a motorbike in Vietnam, you must have a valid Vietnamese driving license OR an International Driving Permit (IDP) that is compliant with Vietnamese law (e.g., a 1968 convention IDP) paired with your home country's license. While you can still rent a bike from us with just your passport (for the deposit), you are fully responsible for all interactions with the police and any legal consequences of driving without a valid license.

We provide you with a copy of the motorbike's "Blue Card" (Cavet - registration paper) and a signed rental contract. This is the standard procedure for rentals in Hanoi. You must keep this copy and the contract with you at all times while driving. This proves you are in legal possession of the scooter.
